Weird Science is a 1985 American science-fiction comedy film written and
directed by John Hughes. Two high-school students use a computer to create an
artificial woman who helps them gain confidence. The film is a product of the
1980s teen-comedy era that Hughes helped define.

The Breakfast Club, also directed by John Hughes and released in 1985, follows
five students in Saturday detention. It is widely cited as a defining film of
its generation.

A "practical effect" is a special effect produced physically on set, rather
than with computer-generated imagery. Many 1980s films relied heavily on
practical effects.
