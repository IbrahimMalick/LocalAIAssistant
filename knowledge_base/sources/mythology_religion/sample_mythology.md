In Greek mythology, Zeus is the king of the gods and ruler of Mount Olympus,
associated with the sky and thunder. His Roman counterpart is Jupiter.

Norse mythology features Odin, the chief god associated with wisdom, war, and
poetry, and Thor, the thunder god who wields the hammer Mjolnir.

In Egyptian mythology, Ra is a sun god often depicted with a falcon head, and
Anubis is associated with mummification and the afterlife.

A "pantheon" is the collective set of gods worshipped within a particular
religion or mythological tradition.
