# Palmistry — Reading the Hand

Palmistry (chiromancy) reads the hands for insight into character and life
patterns. Traditional meanings in original wording. Treat as intuitive guidance
and entertainment, not fortune-telling of fixed events.

## The major lines

### Heart line
Runs across the top of the palm, below the fingers. Governs love, emotion, and
relationships.
- Long, curved: warm, expressive, romantic.
- Straight and short: reserved, guards the heart, values independence.
- Broken or chained: emotional ups and downs, past hurts.
- Deep and clear: steady, sincere feelings.

### Head line
Runs across the middle of the palm. Governs thinking, learning, and
decision-making.
- Long: thorough, analytical, thinks things through.
- Short: decisive, prefers action to overthinking.
- Curved/sloping: creative, imaginative.
- Straight: practical, logical.
- Joined to the life line at the start: cautious, close family ties early on.

### Life line
Curves around the base of the thumb. Governs vitality, energy, and life
changes — NOT lifespan.
- Deep and long: strong vitality, resilience.
- Faint: lower physical energy, sensitivity.
- Wide arc: warmth, enthusiasm, generosity of spirit.
- Close to the thumb: caution, conserved energy.
- Breaks: major life changes or shifts in direction.

### Fate line (not everyone has one)
Runs vertically up the palm toward the middle finger. Governs career, life
path, and outside influences.
- Strong and clear: a focused sense of direction.
- Faint or absent: a self-directed, changeable path (not a bad sign).
- Breaks and restarts: career changes, reinventions.

## The mounts
Fleshy pads on the palm, each named for a planet, each a character theme.
- Mount of Venus (base of thumb): love, warmth, vitality, sensuality.
- Mount of Jupiter (below index): ambition, leadership, confidence.
- Mount of Saturn (below middle): responsibility, discipline, seriousness.
- Mount of Apollo/Sun (below ring): creativity, charisma, success.
- Mount of Mercury (below little finger): communication, wit, business sense.
- Mounts of Mars (two, sides of palm): courage, resilience, drive.
- Mount of the Moon (outer base): imagination, intuition, emotion.
Well-developed = the trait is strong; flat = the trait is subtler.

## Hand shapes (the elements)
- Earth hand: square palm, short fingers — practical, grounded, reliable.
- Air hand: square palm, long fingers — intellectual, communicative, curious.
- Water hand: long palm, long fingers — sensitive, intuitive, emotional.
- Fire hand: long palm, short fingers — energetic, passionate, bold.

## Fingers and thumb
- Long fingers: detail-oriented, patient. Short fingers: big-picture, fast.
- Flexible thumb: adaptable, generous. Stiff thumb: stubborn, dependable.
- Index (Jupiter): ego, ambition. Middle (Saturn): responsibility.
- Ring (Apollo): creativity, self-expression. Little (Mercury): communication.

## Reading tips
- The dominant hand shows the present and choices made; the other hand shows
  potential and inherited traits.
- Read the hand as a whole story, and always tie observations back to the
  person's actual situation and choices — hands describe tendencies, not fate.
