# Astrology — Signs, Planets, Houses

Western astrology reads the sky's patterns as a mirror for character and timing.
Traditional meanings in original wording. Intuitive guidance, not prediction of
fixed events.

## The twelve zodiac signs

- Aries (Mar 21–Apr 19), fire, cardinal: bold, direct, pioneering, impatient.
- Taurus (Apr 20–May 20), earth, fixed: steady, sensual, loyal, stubborn.
- Gemini (May 21–Jun 20), air, mutable: curious, quick, sociable, restless.
- Cancer (Jun 21–Jul 22), water, cardinal: nurturing, sensitive, protective, moody.
- Leo (Jul 23–Aug 22), fire, fixed: warm, generous, proud, dramatic.
- Virgo (Aug 23–Sep 22), earth, mutable: precise, helpful, analytical, self-critical.
- Libra (Sep 23–Oct 22), air, cardinal: fair, charming, relational, indecisive.
- Scorpio (Oct 23–Nov 21), water, fixed: intense, loyal, perceptive, secretive.
- Sagittarius (Nov 22–Dec 21), fire, mutable: adventurous, honest, optimistic, blunt.
- Capricorn (Dec 22–Jan 19), earth, cardinal: disciplined, ambitious, patient, reserved.
- Aquarius (Jan 20–Feb 18), air, fixed: independent, inventive, humane, detached.
- Pisces (Feb 19–Mar 20), water, mutable: compassionate, intuitive, dreamy, escapist.

## The elements and modalities
- Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): energy, passion, initiative.
- Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): practicality, stability, the material.
- Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): intellect, communication, ideas.
- Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): emotion, intuition, depth.
- Cardinal signs initiate; fixed signs sustain; mutable signs adapt.

## The planets (what they govern)
- Sun: core self, identity, vitality.
- Moon: emotions, instincts, inner needs.
- Mercury: mind, communication, learning.
- Venus: love, beauty, values, pleasure.
- Mars: drive, desire, action, anger.
- Jupiter: growth, luck, expansion, meaning.
- Saturn: discipline, limits, responsibility, time.
- Uranus: change, rebellion, sudden shifts.
- Neptune: dreams, intuition, illusion, spirituality.
- Pluto: transformation, power, deep change.

## The twelve houses (areas of life)
1. Self, identity, appearance.
2. Money, values, possessions.
3. Communication, siblings, short trips.
4. Home, family, roots.
5. Creativity, romance, children, play.
6. Work, health, daily routine.
7. Partnership, marriage, others.
8. Intimacy, shared resources, transformation.
9. Travel, philosophy, higher learning.
10. Career, reputation, public life.
11. Friends, groups, hopes.
12. The unconscious, solitude, endings.

## Key ideas for a reading
- Sun sign = core identity; Moon sign = emotional nature; Rising/Ascendant =
  how one meets the world. All three together are richer than the Sun alone.
- Aspects (angles between planets) show how energies cooperate or clash:
  conjunction (blended), trine/sextile (easy flow), square/opposition (tension).
- A transit is a current planetary movement touching the birth chart — used for
  timing themes, not guaranteeing events.

## Reading tips
- Personalise: ask for a birth date (and time/place if going deep) rather than
  assuming from the Sun sign alone.
- Describe tendencies and timing themes; leave room for choice.
