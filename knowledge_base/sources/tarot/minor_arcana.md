# Tarot — The Minor Arcana

The 56 Minor Arcana cards describe everyday situations, feelings, and events.
Four suits, each tied to a domain of life. Meanings in original wording.

- **Wands** — energy, drive, passion, work, creativity, action (element: fire).
- **Cups** — emotions, love, relationships, intuition (element: water).
- **Swords** — thoughts, conflict, truth, communication, stress (element: air).
- **Pentacles** — money, work, body, home, the material world (element: earth).

The court cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King) can mean people, personalities, or
stages of mastery: Page = beginning/message, Knight = pursuit/action,
Queen = inward mastery/nurture, King = outward mastery/authority.

## Suit of Wands (fire — drive & passion)
- Ace of Wands — upright: spark, inspiration, new venture; reversed: delays, lost drive.
- Two of Wands — upright: planning, first steps, wider horizons; reversed: fear of the unknown, playing small.
- Three of Wands — upright: expansion, foresight, ships coming in; reversed: delays, obstacles abroad.
- Four of Wands — upright: celebration, home, milestones; reversed: instability, a muted celebration.
- Five of Wands — upright: competition, friction, sparring; reversed: conflict avoided or resolved.
- Six of Wands — upright: victory, recognition, confidence; reversed: ego, a fall from favour.
- Seven of Wands — upright: defending your position, standing firm; reversed: overwhelmed, giving ground.
- Eight of Wands — upright: speed, movement, news, momentum; reversed: delays, frustration.
- Nine of Wands — upright: resilience, last stand, guarded; reversed: exhaustion, defensiveness.
- Ten of Wands — upright: burden, overload, responsibility; reversed: delegating, releasing weight.
- Page of Wands — upright: curiosity, a spark of news; reversed: hesitation, scattered ideas.
- Knight of Wands — upright: bold action, adventure; reversed: recklessness, haste.
- Queen of Wands — upright: warm confidence, magnetism; reversed: self-doubt, insecurity.
- King of Wands — upright: visionary leadership, drive; reversed: impulsiveness, domineering.

## Suit of Cups (water — emotion & love)
- Ace of Cups — upright: new love, open heart, emotional beginning; reversed: blocked feelings, emptiness.
- Two of Cups — upright: partnership, mutual attraction, connection; reversed: imbalance, a rift.
- Three of Cups — upright: friendship, celebration, community; reversed: overindulgence, gossip, isolation.
- Four of Cups — upright: apathy, missed offers, reevaluation; reversed: new interest, accepting an offer.
- Five of Cups — upright: grief, regret, focus on loss; reversed: acceptance, moving on, what remains.
- Six of Cups — upright: nostalgia, reunion, innocence; reversed: stuck in the past, moving forward.
- Seven of Cups — upright: too many choices, illusions, fantasy; reversed: clarity, decisive choice.
- Eight of Cups — upright: walking away, seeking more, departure; reversed: fear of change, drifting.
- Nine of Cups — upright: contentment, wishes fulfilled; reversed: dissatisfaction, hollow pleasures.
- Ten of Cups — upright: emotional fulfilment, family harmony; reversed: fractured harmony, unmet ideals.
- Page of Cups — upright: a tender message, creativity, sensitivity; reversed: emotional immaturity.
- Knight of Cups — upright: romance, a heartfelt offer; reversed: moodiness, unrealistic ideals.
- Queen of Cups — upright: compassion, emotional depth, intuition; reversed: over-giving, martyrdom.
- King of Cups — upright: emotional balance, calm control; reversed: suppressed feeling, moodiness.

## Suit of Swords (air — mind & conflict)
- Ace of Swords — upright: clarity, breakthrough, truth; reversed: confusion, misused force.
- Two of Swords — upright: stalemate, hard choice, avoidance; reversed: indecision breaking, information revealed.
- Three of Swords — upright: heartbreak, painful truth, grief; reversed: healing, forgiveness, releasing pain.
- Four of Swords — upright: rest, recovery, pause; reversed: restlessness, burnout, needing to recharge.
- Five of Swords — upright: conflict, winning at a cost, tension; reversed: reconciliation, ending conflict.
- Six of Swords — upright: transition, moving to calmer water; reversed: unfinished business, resistance.
- Seven of Swords — upright: strategy, stealth, getting away with it; reversed: coming clean, exposure.
- Eight of Swords — upright: feeling trapped, self-imposed limits; reversed: freeing yourself, new perspective.
- Nine of Swords — upright: anxiety, worry, sleepless nights; reversed: fears easing, hope returning.
- Ten of Swords — upright: painful ending, rock bottom, betrayal; reversed: recovery, the worst is over.
- Page of Swords — upright: curiosity, watchfulness, new ideas; reversed: gossip, scattered thinking.
- Knight of Swords — upright: fast action, ambition, directness; reversed: haste, aggression.
- Queen of Swords — upright: clear-eyed honesty, independence; reversed: coldness, harsh judgment.
- King of Swords — upright: intellect, authority, fair judgment; reversed: manipulation, cold logic.

## Suit of Pentacles (earth — money & the material)
- Ace of Pentacles — upright: opportunity, new resource, prosperity; reversed: missed chance, insecurity.
- Two of Pentacles — upright: juggling, balance, adaptability; reversed: overwhelm, dropped balls.
- Three of Pentacles — upright: teamwork, skill, collaboration; reversed: discord, lack of teamwork.
- Four of Pentacles — upright: security, saving, holding on; reversed: greed, or loosening a tight grip.
- Five of Pentacles — upright: hardship, worry, feeling left out; reversed: recovery, help arriving.
- Six of Pentacles — upright: generosity, giving and receiving; reversed: strings attached, imbalance.
- Seven of Pentacles — upright: patience, long-term investment; reversed: impatience, poor return.
- Eight of Pentacles — upright: diligence, mastery, craftsmanship; reversed: cutting corners, lost focus.
- Nine of Pentacles — upright: self-sufficiency, earned comfort; reversed: overwork, financial worry.
- Ten of Pentacles — upright: legacy, wealth, family stability; reversed: instability, family tension.
- Page of Pentacles — upright: study, a practical opportunity; reversed: procrastination, missed lessons.
- Knight of Pentacles — upright: reliability, steady effort; reversed: stagnation, dullness.
- Queen of Pentacles — upright: nurturing, practical care, abundance; reversed: over-focus on work or money.
- King of Pentacles — upright: prosperity, stability, provider; reversed: materialism, controlling with money.
