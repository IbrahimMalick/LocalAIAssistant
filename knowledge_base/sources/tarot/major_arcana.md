# Tarot — The Major Arcana

The 22 Major Arcana cards represent life's big themes, turning points, and
archetypal forces. In a reading they point to the deeper current beneath a
situation. Meanings below are written in original wording from the traditional
Rider-Waite-Smith system.

## 0 — The Fool
Upright: new beginnings, leaps of faith, spontaneity, innocence, open roads.
Reversed: recklessness, hesitation, being naive, ignoring risk.
Reading note: a fresh start is available; the question is whether the person
steps forward with trust or freezes at the edge.

## I — The Magician
Upright: personal power, focus, resourcefulness, manifesting intention, skill.
Reversed: manipulation, scattered energy, untapped talent, self-deception.
Reading note: they already have the tools; alignment of will and action is the
theme.

## II — The High Priestess
Upright: intuition, the unseen, secrets, inner knowing, patience.
Reversed: ignored intuition, hidden agendas, surface-level answers.
Reading note: the truth is felt before it's spoken. Encourage listening inward.

## III — The Empress
Upright: nurturing, abundance, creativity, sensuality, growth.
Reversed: creative block, smothering, neglect of self-care, dependence.
Reading note: something is ready to be grown or cared for.

## IV — The Emperor
Upright: structure, authority, stability, discipline, protection.
Reversed: control issues, rigidity, domination, or a lack of boundaries.
Reading note: order and boundaries are the lesson.

## V — The Hierophant
Upright: tradition, guidance, shared values, institutions, mentorship.
Reversed: rebellion, breaking convention, seeking your own path.
Reading note: conform or forge your own way — that tension is present.

## VI — The Lovers
Upright: union, choices, alignment of values, partnership, harmony.
Reversed: misalignment, avoidance, a difficult choice, disharmony.
Reading note: a values-based decision, often but not only in love.

## VII — The Chariot
Upright: willpower, determination, victory through control, forward drive.
Reversed: loss of direction, opposition, scattered effort.
Reading note: momentum is possible if they hold the reins.

## VIII — Strength
Upright: courage, patience, gentle control, inner resilience, compassion.
Reversed: self-doubt, raw emotion, depleted resolve.
Reading note: soft power beats force here.

## IX — The Hermit
Upright: introspection, solitude, inner guidance, searching for truth.
Reversed: isolation, withdrawal, or refusing needed reflection.
Reading note: answers come from stepping back, not pushing harder.

## X — Wheel of Fortune
Upright: cycles, turning points, luck, change, fate in motion.
Reversed: bad timing, resistance to change, a downturn to ride out.
Reading note: the situation is shifting; adaptability matters.

## XI — Justice
Upright: fairness, truth, cause and effect, accountability, clear decisions.
Reversed: unfairness, avoidance of responsibility, imbalance.
Reading note: honesty and consequences are in play.

## XII — The Hanged Man
Upright: surrender, new perspective, pause, letting go, sacrifice.
Reversed: stalling, martyrdom, resistance to letting go.
Reading note: a needed pause or a change of viewpoint.

## XIII — Death
Upright: endings, transformation, release, a necessary close, renewal.
Reversed: clinging to the past, fear of change, stagnation.
Reading note: rarely literal — something must end for the next thing to begin.

## XIV — Temperance
Upright: balance, moderation, patience, blending, healing.
Reversed: excess, imbalance, impatience, conflict.
Reading note: the middle path and steady blending are favoured.

## XV — The Devil
Upright: attachment, temptation, unhealthy patterns, feeling trapped.
Reversed: breaking free, reclaiming power, facing a hard truth.
Reading note: the chains are often looser than they feel.

## XVI — The Tower
Upright: sudden upheaval, revelation, collapse of false structures, shock.
Reversed: fear of change, delaying the inevitable, softened disruption.
Reading note: what falls was not built on truth.

## XVII — The Star
Upright: hope, renewal, healing, inspiration, calm after the storm.
Reversed: discouragement, lost faith, disconnection.
Reading note: gentle optimism and recovery.

## XVIII — The Moon
Upright: illusion, intuition, uncertainty, hidden things, dreams.
Reversed: confusion clearing, truth surfacing, releasing fear.
Reading note: not everything is as it appears; trust the gut, verify the facts.

## XIX — The Sun
Upright: joy, clarity, success, vitality, warmth.
Reversed: temporary clouds, delayed happiness, low energy.
Reading note: one of the brightest cards — genuine positivity.

## XX — Judgement
Upright: reckoning, awakening, calling, self-evaluation, renewal.
Reversed: self-doubt, avoiding a reckoning, harsh self-judgment.
Reading note: a moment of honest self-assessment and rebirth.

## XXI — The World
Upright: completion, wholeness, achievement, integration, fulfilment.
Reversed: unfinished business, loose ends, a delay before closure.
Reading note: a cycle completing; a threshold crossed.
