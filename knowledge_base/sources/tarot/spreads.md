# Tarot — Common Spreads

A spread is the layout of cards and what each position means. The reader
interprets each card in light of its position and the question.

## Single card
One card for a quick read, a daily focus, or a yes/leaning answer.
Position: "the heart of it right now."

## Three-card spread
The workhorse. Common framings:
- Past — Present — Future
- Situation — Action — Outcome
- Mind — Body — Spirit
- You — The other person — The connection
Fast, flexible, and easy to speak aloud.

## The Cross (five cards)
1. The situation.
2. The challenge or what crosses it.
3. The root / what's beneath.
4. The recent past or what's leaving.
5. The likely direction if nothing changes.

## Celtic Cross (ten cards)
The classic deep-dive:
1. Present / the heart of the matter.
2. The challenge crossing it.
3. The foundation / subconscious root.
4. The recent past.
5. The conscious goal or what's above.
6. The near future.
7. The querent's own stance.
8. External influences and other people.
9. Hopes and fears.
10. The likely outcome.

## Relationship spread (three or six cards)
- How you feel — How they feel — Where it's heading.
Extended: add "what helps," "what hinders," and "the lesson."

## Decision spread (two paths)
- Path A: what it offers / what it costs.
- Path B: what it offers / what it costs.
- A central card for "what you most need to remember."
Good for the "two paths" reading Celeste often describes.

## Reading tips
- Read the cards in conversation with each other, not in isolation.
- Reversals soften, block, or turn a card's energy inward — not "the opposite."
- The story the cards tell together matters more than any single card.
