# Numerology — The Meaning of Numbers

Numerology reads numbers derived from names and birth dates as a mirror for
character and themes. Traditional meanings in original wording. Intuitive
guidance, not fixed prediction.

## Core number meanings (1–9)
- 1 — leadership, independence, initiative, new starts. Shadow: ego, isolation.
- 2 — partnership, balance, diplomacy, sensitivity. Shadow: indecision, over-dependence.
- 3 — creativity, expression, joy, communication. Shadow: scattered, superficial.
- 4 — stability, discipline, hard work, structure. Shadow: rigidity, stubbornness.
- 5 — freedom, change, adventure, adaptability. Shadow: restlessness, excess.
- 6 — nurturing, responsibility, home, harmony. Shadow: meddling, self-sacrifice.
- 7 — introspection, analysis, spirituality, wisdom. Shadow: aloofness, doubt.
- 8 — ambition, power, material success, authority. Shadow: control, workaholism.
- 9 — compassion, completion, humanitarianism, wisdom. Shadow: martyrdom, letting go.

## Master numbers
- 11 — the intuitive: heightened insight, inspiration, sensitivity. Carries the
  energy of 2 amplified.
- 22 — the master builder: turning big visions into reality. Carries 4 amplified.
- 33 — the master teacher: compassion and service at scale. Carries 6 amplified.
Master numbers are usually not reduced further.

## The Life Path number
The most-used number, derived from the full birth date. It describes the
overall theme of a person's journey.

How to calculate:
1. Reduce the month, day, and year each to a single digit (or a master number).
2. Add those results together.
3. Reduce the total to a single digit — unless it is 11, 22, or 33.

Example: a birth date of July 16, 1990 →
- Month: 7
- Day: 16 → 1 + 6 = 7
- Year: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
- Total: 7 + 7 + 1 = 15 → 1 + 5 = 6
- Life Path = 6 (nurturing, responsibility, home).

## Other useful numbers
- Expression/Destiny number: from the full birth name — natural talents and how
  one expresses them.
- Soul Urge/Heart's Desire: from the vowels in the name — inner motivations.
- Personality number: from the consonants — how others first perceive you.
- Personal Year: current birth month + day + current year, reduced — the theme
  of the year the person is in.

## Reading tips
- Ask for a birth date to compute the Life Path; it personalises the reading
  instantly.
- Pair a number's meaning with the person's actual situation, and describe its
  light and shadow honestly.
