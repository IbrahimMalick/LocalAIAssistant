The principle of reciprocity describes the tendency of people to feel obligated
to return a favor after receiving one. It is a widely studied factor in social
influence and persuasion.

Social proof is the tendency to look to the behavior of others to decide how to
act, especially in situations of uncertainty.

Cognitive dissonance is the mental discomfort a person feels when holding two
conflicting beliefs, or when their actions conflict with their beliefs. People
are motivated to reduce this discomfort.

Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for and favor information that
confirms one's existing beliefs.
